About WordWhiz 🌙

🌟 What is WordWhiz?

WordWhiz is an interactive web platform engineered to help young students improve their English spelling, build confidence in reading, and master standard British English (Received Pronunciation).

🎯 How Spelling Correction Works

When you enter a word, our Python Flask backend leverages natural language algorithms to evaluate edit distances, common phonetic slips, and letter permutations to offer the best correction.

🎙️ Speech & Voice Technology

WordWhiz uses browser-native speech recognition to convert your voice into text without sending your raw audio to third-party ad networks, preserving privacy and delivering high speed.
